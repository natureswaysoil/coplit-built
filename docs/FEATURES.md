# Feature Overview - Nature's Way Soil Website Improvements

## 🎥 Video Content

### Hero Video
**Purpose**: Educate visitors about soil health and synthetic fertilizer problems

**Key Messages**:
- Mycorrhizal fungi partnerships with 90% of plants
- Natural nutrient exchange through "wood-wide web"
- How synthetic fertilizers disrupt soil biology
- 84% reduction in beneficial microbes from chemicals
- Benefits of organic alternatives

**Impact**: Increases time on site, builds trust, educates customers

### Product Videos
**Purpose**: Explain each product's benefits and usage

**Content**:
- Problem the product solves
- Scientific basis for effectiveness
- Key features and benefits
- Application instructions
- Visual demonstrations

**Impact**: Reduces support questions, increases conversion rates

## 💬 AI-Powered Chat

### Capabilities
- **Product Recommendations**: Suggests products based on customer needs
- **Soil Science Education**: Explains complex concepts simply
- **Application Guidance**: Provides usage instructions
- **Troubleshooting**: Helps diagnose plant/soil issues
- **Order Support**: Answers questions about shipping, returns, etc.

### Intelligence
- Powered by OpenAI GPT-4
- Trained on all product information

- Provides accurate, helpful responses

### Analytics

- Track common questions
